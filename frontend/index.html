Spotter AI — Weather-Aware Commercial Truck Routing

Professional corridor meteorological risk assessment and freight safety navigation.

Predict Road Hazards Along Commercial Freight Corridors

Spotter AI provides commercial truck drivers, fleet operators, and freight dispatchers with weather intelligence matched to road corridors. Unlike standard weather apps that display current radar for a static city, Spotter AI calculates the exact Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) for each 10, 25, or 50-mile segment of a route and pairs it with real-time numerical weather predictions.

Key Safety & Routing Capabilities

  • Crosswind & Blowover Overrides: Calculates trailer rollover danger based on gross load weight (15,000 lbs empty up to 80,000 lbs loaded).
  • Corridor Comparison: Compares up to 3 driving alternatives, ranking them lexicographically by miles of severe wind, snow, and rain exposure.
  • 48-Hour Corridor Heatmaps: 49-frame hourly forecasts visualizing moving storm fronts, snow bands, and wind fields.
  • Verified Forecast Data: Powered by Open-Meteo ensemble weather models and Mapbox navigation networks—no mock or simulated data.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does weather-aware truck routing prevent freight delays?

By identifying hazardous segments before departure, drivers can alter departure times, choose alternative corridors, or avoid known mountain pass blizzards and high-wind corridors like I-80 in Wyoming or I-70 through Colorado.

What qualifies as a 'No Travel' safety status?

Conditions with extreme sustained crosswinds exceeding rollover thresholds, heavy blizzard snowfall rates, or severe freezing precipitation cause Spotter AI to flag a corridor as ineligible for safe commercial travel.
